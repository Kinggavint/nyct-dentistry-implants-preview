Services in Mount Kisco

What you can have done in Mount Kisco.

These are the treatments our Mount Kisco office offers. Which one is right for you is decided at an exam, after we look at your teeth, gums and goals.

  • Dental Implants

    Permanent tooth replacement that looks and feels natural. Single teeth or full arches can be restored with implants.

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Whitening, veneers and other personalized treatments to enhance your smile.

  • Preventive Care

    Regular cleanings and exams. Catching problems early saves you time and money.

  • Emergency Dentistry

    Relief for dental pain and injuries. Call the office and tell the team what is going on.

  • Porcelain Veneers

    Custom-crafted porcelain shells for a natural-looking smile, often completed in a few visits.

  • Dental Crowns

    Custom-fitted crowns that strengthen a weakened tooth while keeping a natural look and function.

Your doctors

Two doctors, one team.

Dr. GiTae Kwon is an implant surgeon with a master's degree in dental implantology who also teaches implant surgery to other dentists. He values communication and a calm bedside manner, and his focus includes general, cosmetic and implant dentistry, full mouth reconstruction and All-on-X treatment.

Dr. Byungchul Park has cared for Connecticut patients since 2015 and practices across Fairfield and Litchfield counties in Connecticut and Northern Westchester, New York. Patients know him for a calm, patient and exceptionally gentle approach, with clear communication and close attention to comfort.

Both doctors invest in modern diagnostic tools and treatment equipment, so procedures are precise and comfortable. Meet the doctors and team →

Common questions

Before you visit.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Each office is in network with its own list of plans, and coverage can vary by employer. See the Mount Kisco plans on our insurance page, then call the office so we can verify your benefits before your visit.

What are the office hours?

The hours are listed above, and they differ from day to day. Call 914-666-5225 if you are not sure the office is open when you plan to come.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Schedule Now button on this page, or call the office and the team will help you find a time.

I am in pain. Can I be seen?

We make room for patients in pain whenever we can. Call the Mount Kisco office and tell the team what is happening.
