Inside the office

A calm place to be seen.

Our Stamford office greets you with a serene, modern atmosphere and comfortable seating. Your dental visit begins the moment you walk through the door, so our team welcomes you warmly and points you to everything you need.

  • Technology

    Our Stamford office uses CBCT 3D scans and intraoral cameras. Intraoral cameras are small handheld wands that capture highly detailed images of your teeth, and CBCT shows your bone and teeth in three dimensions.

  • Comfort

    Our Stamford office has TVs with Netflix and YouTube, tablets, relaxing music and warm towels to freshen up after your appointment.

  • Easy to Visit

    Our Stamford office is open Saturdays, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm, and parking on site is complimentary.

Our mission

Dentistry meant to make you smile.

We want you to experience your boldest, happiest smile yet, so we take time to understand what each patient needs. Dr. Park and Dr. Kwon share a heart for underserved patients and want a modern, accessible dental space for everyone who calls Stamford home.

Our Stamford office accepts Medicaid through HUSKY Health for children and adults, and we offer care for the whole family, from young children to seniors. See the accepted plans and call us to verify your benefits.

Services here include dental implants, full-arch implants, clear aligners, cosmetic dentistry, crowns, dentures, root canal treatment, preventive care and emergency dentistry. See all services →

Common questions

Before you visit.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Each office is in network with its own list of plans, and the Stamford office also accepts Medicaid through HUSKY Health for children and adults. See the Stamford plans on our insurance page, then call the office so we can verify your benefits before your visit.

What are the office hours?

The hours are listed above, and the office is open Saturdays, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm. Call 203-883-4451 if you are not sure the office is open when you plan to come.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Schedule Now button on this page, or call the office and the team will help you find a time.

I am in pain. Can I be seen?

Call 203-883-4451 and tell the team what is happening. We often see patients in pain the same day.

Looking for a new dental home in Stamford?

We are accepting new patients. Book online or call, and see our service area for the towns we welcome patients from.
