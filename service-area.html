Why patients travel

Worth the drive.

Whether you are missing one tooth, several teeth or all of them, dental implants may be a way to restore your smile and your oral health. Many patients find that the right team matters more than the shortest drive.

Our doctors work together. Dr. GiTae Kwon is an implant surgeon who also teaches implant surgery to other dentists, and Dr. Byungchul Park is known for gentle, comfort-first care. Your plan starts with an exam and a clear conversation about your options, so you know what each choice involves before any work begins.

Our Stamford office is open Saturdays and offers complimentary parking, which helps if you are coming from a few towns away. Our offices also differ in the insurance plans they accept, so see the accepted plans before you choose.

What to expect

Your first visit.

  1. An exam and imaging

    We look at your teeth, gums and bone. At our Stamford office this includes CBCT 3D scans and intraoral cameras.

  2. A plan in plain words

    The doctors explain your options, the steps involved and the timing. Your exam decides what is right for you.

  3. A clear cost estimate

    We check your benefits and give you a written estimate before treatment. See insurance and financing for plans, specials and payment options.

Common questions

Coming from out of town.

Which office should I choose?

Most patients choose the office closest to home. If you live in Fairfield County, CT, or in Port Chester or New Rochelle, NY, that is usually our Stamford office. If you live elsewhere in Westchester County, it is usually our Mount Kisco office, and our Kent office serves Kent and northwest Connecticut.

Do all offices offer dental implants?

Yes. Dental implants are available at all three offices, and your plan begins with an exam. Visit dental implants to learn how the process works.

Can I see a doctor at a different office?

Both doctors care for patients across our offices. Call the office you would like to visit and the team will help you find a time that works.

Will my insurance work at every office?

Not always. Each office is in network with its own list of plans, so check the accepted plans and call us to verify your benefits.
