The People Behind Your Care

An implant surgeon who teaches his craft, a dentist known for his calm and gentle care, and a team that makes every visit feel personal. Clear explanations, unhurried visits and careful hands.

Dr. GiTae Kwon, DDS MSc.

Implant surgeon and educator

Dr. Kwon practices comprehensive general, cosmetic and implant dentistry, from everyday restorative care to smile makeovers, porcelain veneers, advanced implant surgery, sinus augmentation, full-mouth rehabilitation and All-on-X treatment. He graduated summa cum laude from New York University, earned his DDS at NYU College of Dentistry, and in 2023 earned a Master's degree in Dental Implantology and Advanced Surgical Extractions from UniCamillus in Rome, where he is a visiting professor.

He teaches fellow dentists as faculty for the GIA Implant Seminar Live Surgery Course in Tijuana, Mexico, and since 2024 has been invited to lecture across North America on implant surgery and sinus augmentation.

Dr. Byungchul Park.

Caring for Connecticut patients since 2015

Dr. Park has been caring for patients in Connecticut since 2015 and now practices across Fairfield County and Litchfield County, Connecticut, as well as Northern Westchester, New York. Having also lived in New York and New Jersey, he has strong roots throughout the Tri-State area.

He is known for his calm, patient and exceptionally gentle approach. He understands that dental treatment can be stressful for many patients, and his bedside manner, clear communication and attention to comfort are defining features of his care. His approach is especially well suited for patients who have had difficult dental experiences in the past, or who benefit from a more patient and reassuring style of care.

His philosophy centers on comprehensive, thoughtful dentistry with an emphasis on long-term function, patient comfort and individualized treatment planning. Whether providing routine restorative care or managing more complex needs, he takes the time to understand each patient's concerns, explain the options and deliver care with precision and consistency.

Dr. Park values the long-term relationships he builds with his patients and believes trust is built through consistency, communication and attention to detail. He strives to make every visit feel organized, respectful and personal.
