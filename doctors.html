Our Team

Two Doctors, One Way of Caring

Dr. Kwon and Dr. Park are colleagues, friends and teammates. Patients see the same thing in both: clear explanations, unhurried visits and careful hands.

Dr. GiTae Kwon, DDS MSc

Implant surgeon and educator

Dr. Kwon focuses on dental implant surgery, smile makeovers, full-mouth reconstruction and All-on-X treatment. He graduated summa cum laude from New York University, earned his DDS at NYU College of Dentistry, and in 2023 completed a Master's degree in Dental Implantology and Advanced Surgical Extractions at the University of Camillus in Rome, where he is a visiting professor.

He teaches implant surgery to other dentists as part-time faculty at the Gangnam International Implant Academy and lectures across North and Central America.

Dr. Byungchul Park

Your Connecticut dentist since 2015

Dr. Park has been caring for Connecticut patients since 2015. Before that he lived in New York and New Jersey, so he knows the Tristate area well.

Ask his patients and you will hear the same thing: he has the gentlest hands you could ask for in a dentist. Whatever treatment you need, he works to make it as comfortable as possible, and because he knows your time is precious, he aims to give you the best care you can find in Fairfield County.

When he is not in the office, you will find him at Chelsea Piers in Stamford, playing soccer with Dr. Kwon and their teammates. Is he as good a soccer player as he is a dentist? Ask him at your next visit.

The People Who Welcome You

From the Front Desk to the Chair

Down-to-earth hospitality is part of the care. Meet two of the people you will see at our Stamford office.

Maggie

Receptionist

Maggie has made dentistry her passion, and patient care is her first priority. She is your link between the office and your visit, organized, warm and bilingual in Spanish, so Spanish-speaking patients can talk with us in their own language.
