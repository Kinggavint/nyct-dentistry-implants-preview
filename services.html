Implants

Replace one tooth, or a whole smile.

Planned and placed by Dr. Kwon, an implant surgeon who also teaches the procedure.

  • Dental Implants

    A titanium post and a custom crown that looks, feels and works like a natural tooth.

    Learn more about Dental Implants

  • Single-Tooth Implants

    One implant and one crown, without touching the healthy teeth beside it.

    Learn more about Single-Tooth Implants

  • All-on-4 and All-on-X

    A fixed full arch on four or more implants. Unlike a denture, it does not come out.

    Learn more about All-on-4 and All-on-X

  • Dentures

    Full, partial and implant-supported dentures to eat, speak and smile comfortably again.

    Learn more about Dentures
