Implant Dentistry

Dental Implants

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone where a tooth root used to be. Once it has bonded with the bone, it supports a custom crown that looks, feels and works like a natural tooth. Implants can replace one tooth, several teeth, or support a full arch.

Dr. Kwon places implants himself. He holds a Master's degree in Dental Implantology and Advanced Surgical Extractions and teaches implant surgery to other dentists, so your case is planned and performed by the same person.

Who is a candidate

Most adults with a missing tooth and enough healthy bone. At your consultation Dr. Kwon reviews your scans, gums and overall health and tells you plainly whether an implant is the right choice.

Implant Dentistry

Same-Day Dental Implants

For the right patient, the implant and a temporary tooth can be placed in a single visit, so you leave with a complete smile while the implant heals underneath.

Same-day placement is not right for everyone. It depends on bone quality, gum health and how your bite comes together. Dr. Kwon only recommends it when it will hold up to the same long-term standard as a traditional implant.

Full-Mouth Reconstruction

All-on-4, All-on-X and X-on-X

When most or all of the teeth in an upper or lower arch are failing or missing, a fixed bridge supported by four or more implants can replace the whole arch. Unlike a denture, it does not come out.

All-on-4 uses four implants. All-on-X uses more when your bone or bite calls for extra support. Dr. Kwon decides the number from your imaging and exam, and plans the full sequence with you before treatment begins. Full-mouth reconstruction and All-on-X are a central part of his practice.

Cosmetic and Restorative

Smile Makeovers

A smile makeover brings shape, color, proportion and function together in one plan. It may combine veneers, crowns, whitening and restorative work, designed as a single result rather than one tooth at a time.

Each makeover is planned as a clinical project as much as a cosmetic one, so the new smile is as strong as it is beautiful. See before and after photos of our own patients.

Cosmetic and Restorative

Veneers and Crowns

Veneers are thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of the teeth to change their shape, length or color.

Crowns cover and protect a tooth that is cracked, heavily filled or weakened, or sit on top of an implant. Modern materials such as zirconia are strong and look natural, and can replace older metal-based crowns that show dark lines at the gum.

Saving Natural Teeth

Root Canal Treatment

When the nerve inside a tooth becomes infected or inflamed, a root canal removes the source of the pain, cleans and seals the inside of the tooth, and lets you keep it. The tooth is usually protected with a crown afterward.

The treatment is done under local anesthesia. Many patients find it feels much like having a filling, and the relief afterward is often immediate.

Treatment Questions

What Patients Ask

How long does an implant last?

An implant placed in healthy bone and cared for well is meant to last for many years, often decades. The crown on top may eventually need replacing, but the implant itself is intended as a long-term solution.

Does getting an implant hurt?

The procedure is done under local anesthesia, and most patients are surprised by how manageable it is. We explain what to expect during healing and how to stay comfortable.

What is the difference between All-on-4 and All-on-X?

All-on-4 supports a full arch on four implants. All-on-X uses more implants when your bone or bite needs extra support. Dr. Kwon chooses the number from your scans.

How many veneers will I need?

It depends on which teeth show when you smile and which ones need correcting. Dr. Kwon decides this with you at your exam and smile design visit.

Do you see children?

We care for patients of all ages, and families are welcome. Call the office to book.
