Common Questions

Before You Call

Still unsure? Call the office closest to you, or use the chat button at the bottom of the page.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with a missing tooth and enough healthy bone are candidates. Dr. Kwon checks your bone, gums and overall health at a consultation before recommending an implant.

Can I get an implant the same day?

Same-day implants are available for patients who meet the clinical criteria. At your consultation, Dr. Kwon reviews your scans and tells you honestly whether same-day placement is right for you.

I am in pain. How soon can you see me?

We make room for patients in pain and often see them the same day. Call the office closest to you and tell the team what is going on.

Do you take my insurance?

Our Mount Kisco and Kent offices are in network with many plans, and each office accepts a different list. See the plans by office, then call so we can check your current benefits.

Can I spread out the cost?

Yes. We offer Cherry payment plans at all three offices, with 0% financing for 12 to 18 months for qualified applicants. Read about financing.
