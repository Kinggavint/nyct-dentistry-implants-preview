What We Do Best

Replacing Teeth and Rebuilding Smiles

Every plan starts with an exam and a real conversation. You will know what we found, what your options are, and what each one involves before any work begins.

01 · Dental Implants

A Missing Tooth, Replaced by the Surgeon Who Teaches the Technique

An implant replaces the root of a missing tooth and supports a crown that looks and works like your own. Dr. Kwon places implants himself and teaches implant surgery to other dentists, so the person planning your case is the person doing the work.

For the right patient, a same-day implant can place the implant and a temporary tooth in one visit. Whether that suits you depends on your bone and gums, which we check first.

Dental implants →

02 · Smile Makeovers

See Your New Smile Before Any Tooth Is Touched

A smile makeover brings veneers, crowns and other restorative work together into one plan. With virtual smile design, you see a digital preview of the result first, so you can ask for changes while they are still easy to make.

Smile makeovers →

03 · Full-Arch Treatment

All-on-4 and All-on-X for a Full Set of Fixed Teeth

When most or all teeth in an arch are failing or missing, a fixed bridge on four or more implants can replace them. Dr. Kwon focuses on full-mouth reconstruction and All-on-X treatment, and decides how many implants you need from your scans, not from a formula.

Common Questions

Before You Call

Still unsure? Call the office closest to you, or use the chat button at the bottom of the page.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with a missing tooth and enough healthy bone are candidates. Dr. Kwon checks your bone, gums and overall health at a consultation before recommending an implant.

Can I get an implant the same day?

Same-day implants are available for patients who meet the clinical criteria. At your consultation, Dr. Kwon reviews your scans and tells you honestly whether same-day placement is right for you.

I am in pain. How soon can you see me?

We make room for patients in pain and often see them the same day. Call the office closest to you and tell the team what is going on.

Do you take my insurance?

Our Mount Kisco and Kent offices are in network with many plans, and each office accepts a different list. See the plans by office, then call so we can check your current benefits.

Can I spread out the cost?

Yes. We offer Cherry payment plans, including 0% options for qualified applicants, and CareCredit. Read about financing.
