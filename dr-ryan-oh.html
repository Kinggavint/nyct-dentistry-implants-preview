His story

Comfortable, welcoming, personal.

Dr. Oh is a compassionate and dedicated general dentist committed to providing personalized, comprehensive care in a comfortable and welcoming environment. He believes that exceptional dentistry is built on clinical excellence, attention to detail, and genuine relationships with his patients.

Dr. Oh strives to make every patient feel heard, comfortable and confident in their dental care. He looks forward to helping each patient achieve and maintain a healthy, confident smile.
