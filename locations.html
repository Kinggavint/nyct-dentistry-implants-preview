Before your visit

Good to know.

I am in pain. Can I be seen today?

Call the office closest to you right away. We make room for patients in pain and often see them the same day.

Can I see the same doctor at any office?

Ask when you book and the team will tell you which doctor is at each office on your preferred day.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your insurance card if you have one, a photo ID, a list of any medications, and any recent X-rays or records from a previous dentist.
