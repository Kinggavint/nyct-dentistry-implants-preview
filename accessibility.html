Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 2026

Dentistry & Implants is committed to making our website and patient information accessible to the widest possible audience. If you have difficulty using this site, please contact us so our team can help you directly.

Accessibility toolbar

Every page has an accessibility toolbar at the bottom right of the screen. It lets you:

  • Make text larger
  • Switch to a high contrast display
  • Underline links so they are easier to spot

Your choices are saved in your browser on your own device, so they carry over as you move between pages.

Keyboard navigation

You can move through the site using the Tab key, and a skip link at the top of each page takes you straight to the main content. Menus, buttons and forms are built to work without a mouse.

Images and structure

Photos on the site have text descriptions (alt text) for people who use screen readers. Pages use headings in order so they are easier to scan and navigate.

Our ongoing work

We keep working to improve the site. Some parts may not yet meet every accessibility guideline, and we welcome your feedback.

Report a barrier or ask for help

If something on the site is hard to use, or you need information in another way, tell us. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service you need.
