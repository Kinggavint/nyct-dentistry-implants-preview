Local dental care

Dental care near Norwalk, CT

If you live near Norwalk, you have options close by. Here is what we offer and how to reach us.

From the Dentistry & Implants team

Care for the whole family

Our team provides general, cosmetic and restorative dentistry in one practice, so you can often stay with the same dentists for most of your needs. Routine care includes cleanings, exams and screenings as well as treatment of cavities, gum problems and emergencies.

Meet your dentists

Dr. GiTae Kwon is an implant surgeon who also teaches implant surgery to other dentists. Dr. Byungchul Park provides gentle, comfort-first care and has practiced in Connecticut since 2015. Learn more on our doctor page.

What we offer

  • Dental implants, from single teeth to full arch.
  • Clear aligners to straighten teeth.
  • Crowns and porcelain veneers.
  • Teeth whitening.
  • Root canal treatment and extractions.
  • Dentures.
  • Emergency dentistry.

Getting here from Norwalk

Norwalk sits right next to Stamford, so our Stamford office is the closest for most Norwalk patients. It has Saturday hours for people who work during the week, and our Kent office is also available. You can see all of our locations and hours, and the towns we serve, on our service area page.

Paying for care

We check your insurance benefits before your visit and give you a written estimate before treatment. Cherry payment plans with 0% financing for 12 to 18 months are available to qualified applicants, and every office has a new patient special. See our insurance and financing page for details.

A practice built around listening

Our approach is simple: listen first, explain plainly and let you decide. We would rather spend a few extra minutes making sure you understand a plan than have you leave with questions. That is true whether you come in for a cleaning or a full smile makeover.

Common questions

Do you take new patients? Yes. Call the office or book a consultation.

Do you accept my insurance? Each office is in network with a different list of plans, so check the insurance page or call us to confirm.

What if I have a dental emergency? Call the nearest office as soon as you can and we will help you decide what to do.

Why patients travel for implants and cosmetic care

Some treatments, like implants, benefit from a dentist who does them often. Dr. Kwon places implants and teaches the technique to other dentists, which is why some patients make the short drive for that part of their care and keep routine visits closer to home if they prefer.

Your first visit

Your first visit is a chance to meet us, talk about your goals and get an exam. We listen first and explain your options plainly. If something needs treatment, you will hear why, what it involves and what it costs, before anything begins.
