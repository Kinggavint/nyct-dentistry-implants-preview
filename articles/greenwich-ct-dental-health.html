Preventive Care for Every Age

Good dental health is mostly quiet, steady habits. Regular cleanings and exams catch small problems, such as early cavities and gum changes, before they turn into larger ones. For children, protective sealants and fluoride treatments help guard growing teeth. For adults, an oral cancer screening is part of a thorough checkup.

Between visits, brushing twice a day, cleaning between your teeth daily and limiting sugary snacks and drinks do most of the work. If you notice bleeding gums, a sensitive tooth or a sore that does not heal, do not wait for your next cleaning. Call us.

When You Need More Than a Cleaning

If a tooth is damaged or missing, you will hear all of your options. We treat extraction as a last resort and try to save a natural tooth first. When a tooth cannot be saved, replacing it with an implant, bridge or denture can restore your bite and your confidence. Dr. Kwon has advanced training in implants and surgical extractions, so complex cases can often be planned and treated in one place.
