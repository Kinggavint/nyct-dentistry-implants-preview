Inside the Office

A Calm Place to Be Seen

Our Stamford office greets you with a serene, modern atmosphere and comfortable seating. Your dental visit begins the moment you walk through the door, so our team welcomes you warmly and points you to everything you need.

  • Technology

    Our Stamford office uses CBCT 3D scans and intraoral cameras. Intraoral cameras are small handheld wands that capture highly detailed images of your teeth, and CBCT shows your bone and teeth in three dimensions.

  • Comfort

    Our Stamford office has TVs with Netflix and YouTube, tablets, relaxing music and warm towels to freshen up after your appointment.

  • Easy to Visit

    Our Stamford office is open Saturdays, 9:00 am to 3:00 pm, and parking on site is complimentary.

Our Mission

Dentistry Meant to Make You Smile

We want you to experience your boldest, happiest smile yet, so we take time to understand what each patient needs. Dr. Park and Dr. Kwon share a heart for underserved patients and want a modern, accessible dental space for everyone who calls Stamford home.

Our Stamford office accepts Medicaid (HUSKY Health) for children under 21, and we offer care for the whole family, from young children to seniors. See the accepted plans and call us to verify your benefits.
