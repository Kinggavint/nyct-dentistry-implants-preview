How it works

The money conversation, handled.

  1. Share your insurance

    When you book, give us your carrier and plan details. We check your benefits before you arrive.

  2. Get a written estimate

    After your exam, you receive an estimate that separates what insurance is expected to pay from what you would pay.

  3. Choose how to pay

    If your treatment goes beyond your coverage, we walk you through payment plans and other options before you commit.

Coverage questions

What insurance covers.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

It depends on your plan. Some plans cover part of an implant as a major procedure. Others exclude implants. We verify your exact benefits and give you a written estimate before treatment.

Are veneers and smile makeovers covered?

Work done purely for appearance is usually not covered. When a crown or veneer is needed for structural reasons, part of the cost may be. We will tell you what applies to your case.

Do you take my insurance?

Call the office and we will check. Many plans offer out-of-network benefits. Call the office and we will check what your plan pays. If you have no insurance, we give you a written estimate before treatment and can talk through Cherry payment plans.

Questions about your coverage?

Call the office closest to you and we will check your benefits before your visit.
