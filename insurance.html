Coverage questions

What insurance covers.

Does dental insurance cover implants?

It depends on your plan. Some plans cover part of an implant as a major procedure. Others exclude implants. We verify your exact benefits and give you a written estimate before treatment.

Are veneers and smile makeovers covered?

Work done purely for appearance is usually not covered. When a crown or veneer is needed for structural reasons, part of the cost may be. We will tell you what applies to your case.

Is my plan accepted?

We participate with most PPO insurance plans. Call the office you plan to visit and we will check what your plan pays. Many plans also offer out-of-network benefits. If you have no insurance, we give you a written estimate before treatment and can talk through Cherry payment plans.

Questions about your coverage?

Call the office closest to you and we will check your benefits before your visit.
