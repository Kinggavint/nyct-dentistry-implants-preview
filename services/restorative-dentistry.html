Restorative dentistry

Repair the Tooth. Restore the Smile.

Cracks, cavities, worn edges and missing teeth can all be repaired.

Schedule NowCall Now

The goal

Rebuild how your teeth look, feel and work.

Restorative dentistry brings back a smile and a bite that have been affected by injury, decay, grinding or tooth loss. The right treatment depends on how much of the tooth is left and what you want from it, and we explain each option before you decide.

Dr. Kwon and Dr. Park care for patients of all ages, including children with a damaged or infected tooth.

Waiting usually makes repairs larger. A small cavity or a chipped edge is simpler to fix today than a broken or infected tooth next year. If something feels off, even if it does not hurt, an exam is the right first step, and you will leave with a clear plan and a written estimate.

Treatments

Options for every situation.

We start with the most conservative treatment that will last.

  • Fillings

    The decayed part of the tooth is removed and the tooth is rebuilt with natural-looking, tooth-colored resin. A filling stops the pain and the spread of the cavity.

  • Crowns

    A crown covers and protects a tooth that is cracked, heavily filled or weakened.

    Dental crowns

  • Root canal therapy

    When a tooth is infected, a root canal can save it.

    Root canal treatment

  • Dental bridges

    Crowns on the teeth beside a gap hold one or more replacement teeth in between. Bridges are fixed in place and do not shift or move like a removable partial.

  • Dentures

    Full and partial dentures replace many missing teeth in an affordable, removable way.

    Dentures

  • Implants

    An implant replaces the root and the crown, without relying on neighboring teeth.

    Dental implants

If gum disease is part of the picture, we also offer periodontal care.

Common questions

Repair questions.

How do I know if I need a filling or a crown?

It depends on how much healthy tooth is left. Small cavities usually need a filling. A tooth that is cracked, heavily filled or weak often needs a crown. An exam and X-rays decide.

Will my repair look natural?

Fillings and crowns are made to match the color and shape of your neighboring teeth.

Is a bridge better than a partial denture?

A bridge is fixed in place and does not shift. A partial denture is removable and often more affordable. We help you weigh both.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We restore damaged, infected or missing teeth in patients of all ages.

What will it cost?

Cost depends on your treatment and coverage. We give you a written estimate. See insurance and financing.
