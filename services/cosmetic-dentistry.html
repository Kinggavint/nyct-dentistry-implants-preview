What It Is

Cosmetic Dentistry, Done Naturally

Cosmetic dentistry changes how your smile looks. If gaps, chips, uneven or misshapen teeth, slightly crowded teeth or stains make you hold back your smile, there is probably a good way to address it.

It is different from restorative dentistry, which repairs damaged or missing teeth to restore your bite. In practice the two overlap often, and we plan them together so the result looks natural and also holds up when you chew.

At our Mount Kisco, Kent and Stamford offices, Dr. GiTae Kwon offers personalized cosmetic care built around your face, your teeth and your goals.

Treatments

What We Offer

Most cosmetic plans use one or two of these, sometimes more.

  • Porcelain veneers

    Thin custom shells bonded to the front of the teeth to change their shape, length or color. Learn about veneers.

  • Teeth whitening

    In-office and take-home options for stains and yellowing, on their own or as part of a makeover. Learn about whitening.

  • Dental bonding

    Tooth-colored resin applied directly to the tooth, then sculpted and polished to repair chips and close small gaps. It is quick, conservative and versatile.

  • Shaping and contouring

    Sometimes called enameloplasty. A thin layer of enamel is smoothed from pointed, uneven or slightly chipped teeth for a more even look.

  • Crowns

    When a tooth is heavily filled, cracked or weak, a crown restores its strength and appearance. Learn about crowns.

  • Gum contouring

    Gums can be reshaped for a more balanced smile line when the gum edges are uneven.

  • Invisalign

    Clear aligners for crooked, gapped or crowded teeth, without metal braces. Learn about Invisalign.

Smile Makeovers

One Plan for the Whole Smile

A smile makeover brings shape, color, proportion and function together in a single plan. It may combine veneers, crowns, whitening, bonding and restorative work, designed as one result rather than one tooth at a time.

Each makeover is planned as a clinical project as much as a cosmetic one, so the new smile is as strong as it is beautiful. The photos below are our own patients, shared with written permission. See more before and after photos.

Virtual Smile Design

See the Plan Before We Begin

Before any tooth is prepared, digital imaging builds a preview of your new smile. You see the proposed result, talk it through with Dr. Kwon, and ask for changes while they are still easy to make.

The preview is a planning tool, not a guarantee. It helps you and your dentist agree on the goal first.

Our team also uses shade matching and precise imaging so restorations blend with your natural teeth.

Questions

What Patients Ask

What is cosmetic dentistry?

It is dental care focused on how your smile looks: the shape, color, size and alignment of your teeth. Common treatments include whitening, bonding, veneers and clear aligners.

Are cosmetic treatments permanent?

It depends on the treatment. Whitening fades over time and can be refreshed. Veneers, bonding and crowns are long-term restorations that often last many years with good care, but they may eventually need repair or replacement.

Do cosmetic procedures hurt?

Most are comfortable. We use local anesthesia when it is needed and explain each step beforehand. Whitening can cause temporary sensitivity, which we can manage.

How long does treatment take?

Whitening and bonding can often be done in a visit or two. Veneers, crowns and full makeovers take several visits because the pieces are made to fit you. Your exam will give you a realistic timeline.

Are results guaranteed?

No treatment can promise a specific outcome. A digital preview and a careful plan help us agree on the goal, and an exam decides what is right for your teeth.

How much does cosmetic work cost?

Cost depends on your case and which treatments you choose. We give you a written estimate before treatment. See insurance and financing for plans, payment options and specials.
