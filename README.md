# Dentistry & Implants: Website Preview

Static preview of the new website for Dentistry & Implants (Dr. GiTae Kwon, DDS MSc, and
Dr. Byungchul Park), with offices in Mount Kisco, NY, Kent, CT and Stamford, CT.

The preview is kept out of search results while it is under review: every page carries
`noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` disallows all crawlers. Both are removed at launch.

## Pages

English: Home, Dr. Kwon, Our Doctors, Services, Before and After, Insurance and Financing,
Locations, Articles (three published), Contact.
Spanish (`es/`): Home, Dr. Kwon, Services, Insurance, Locations. In review by the practice.

Open items for the practice are listed in `REVISIONS.md`.
